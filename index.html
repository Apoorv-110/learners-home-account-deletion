Learners' Home – Account Deletion

Learners' Home allows users to delete their account and associated personal data.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Learners' Home application.
  2. Log in to your account.
  3. Open the Settings section.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the account deletion.

What happens when you delete your account?

When you confirm account deletion, your Learners' Home account and associated user data are deleted according to our data deletion process.

Unable to access the app?

If you cannot access the Learners' Home application, you can contact us and request account deletion.

Account deletion request:

learnershome.app@gmail.com

Data retention

Account data is deleted when the account deletion request is processed, except for information that may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.
